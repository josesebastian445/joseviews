Most agencies pick one platform and force every client into it. I start with what
you actually need. A brochure site that changes twice a year does not need a React
build. A product catalogue with 4,000 SKUs should not be a hand-rolled static site.

I build on **WordPress** when non-technical editors need day-to-day control,
**Next.js** when the site has real application logic behind it, and **Astro** when
raw speed and search performance matter more than anything else.

Whatever the choice, the same rules apply: it loads in under two seconds on a phone
on 4G, it passes Core Web Vitals, and nothing breaks when your marketing manager
uploads a 6 MB photo.
