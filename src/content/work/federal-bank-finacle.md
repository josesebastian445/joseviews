## The situation

Bank branches ran on IBM Finacle with a layer of .NET applications around it. When
something failed, it failed during banking hours with customers at the counter, and
the data involved was about as sensitive as data gets.

## What I did

- Supported Finacle and resolved application issues raised by branch systems.
- Conducted code reviews, debugging and integration testing across the .NET
  applications that sat alongside it.
- Designed and maintained SQL Server databases, and generated the daily and
  analytical reports the branches depended on.
- Handled secure data with proper encryption and decryption procedures, and
  provided remote troubleshooting to branches.

## Why it still matters

This is the job that set the standard for everything since. In banking you do not
push a fix and see what happens — you test it, you have a rollback, and you assume
the data is confidential until proven otherwise.

Most web work does not need that level of rigour. It benefits from it anyway.
